Libby Jenke

Assistant Professor of Political Science, University of Houston

Libby Jenke is a quantitative methodologist specializing in causal inference and experimental methods. She uses eye tracking to obtain direct measures of decision processes, information accrual, and attention, and studies questions of voter behavior with an emphasis on affective polarization. She is currently a Visiting Professor at Harvard University and will be a Visiting Research Fellow at the University of Pennsylvania in 2025–2026.
