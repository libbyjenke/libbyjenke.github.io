Libby Jenke

Quantitative UX Researcher, Meta

I am a quantitative methodologist specializing in causal inference and experimental methods. I use eye tracking to obtain direct measures of decision processes, information accrual, and attention, and study questions of voter behavior with an emphasis on affective polarization. I am currently a Quantitative UX Researcher at Meta. Previously, I was tenured at the University of Houston, and held visiting positions at Harvard University and the University of Pennsylvania.
